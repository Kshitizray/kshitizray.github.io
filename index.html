About Me

I am a B.Tech Mechatronics Engineering student at KIIT University with a strong interest in mechanical design, industrial automation, robotics, and embedded systems. I enjoy designing practical engineering solutions by combining mechanical engineering, electronics, and programming.

I have experience working with CAD software such as SolidWorks and Autodesk Fusion 360, as well as embedded platforms including Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi. During my internship at Tata Steel, I worked on an industrial automation project focused on improving safety and efficiency in steel manufacturing.

My goal is to build innovative automation systems and pursue a career in mechanical design and industrial automation while continuously expanding my technical skills through hands-on projects.
