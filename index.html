About Me

I am a B.Tech Mechatronics Engineering student at KIIT University (previously Diploma in Mechatronics Engineering at NTTF RD Tata Technical Education Centre, CGPA 7.82) with a strong interest in mechanical design, product development, and smart manufacturing systems. I enjoy designing practical engineering solutions by combining mechanical engineering, electronics, and programming.

I have experience working with CAD software such as SolidWorks, Siemens NX, Fusion 360, and MasterCAM, as well as embedded platforms including Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi. As a Junior Project Engineer Intern at COTMAV Robotics & Automation, I worked on the implementation of FANUC industrial robots — supporting installation, mechanical setup, testing, and commissioning for clients including Endurance, Studds Helmets, Highco Engineering Works, Arvind Mill, and RKFL, including robotic welding and spray painting automation systems.

My goal is to build innovative automation systems and pursue a career in mechanical design and industrial automation while continuously expanding my technical skills through hands-on projects.
